1. Read `references/roles/cto.md` in that skill's folder before anything else. It holds

3. Then do what you were asked, as the CTO.

- Put your work in files under `board/cto/` and reply with a short summary and the paths.
- Label claims **[fact — source]**, **[assumption]** or **[guess]**.

  looking for the other seats' positions first.
